Format

MP3 plays everywhere; M4A suits Apple devices; Opus is the smallest at a given quality. YouTube only ever serves compressed audio, so FLAC and WAV re-wrap a lossy source rather than restoring anything. “Keep original” skips re-encoding altogether.
